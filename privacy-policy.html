Privacy Policy

We Do Not Collect Your Data

SoulScript does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information. No account is required. No login. No tracking. Your privacy is completely safe.

📋 Overview

SoulScript ("the App") is a daily quotes and wallpapers application developed by Ashish. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app. In short — we don't collect any of it.

🚫 Information We Do NOT Collect

SoulScript does not collect any of the following:

  • Name, email address, or any personal details
  • Phone number or device identifiers
  • Location data
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Photos or media files (except saving to your own gallery)
  • Microphone or camera data
  • Browsing history or usage analytics
  • Financial or payment information
⚙️ How The App Works

SoulScript loads daily quotes from a publicly accessible Google Sheets document. This is a one-way connection — the app only reads quote content. No user data is sent to or stored on any server. All quote saving happens locally on your device only.

🌐 Internet Permission

SoulScript uses internet access solely to fetch daily quotes from Google Sheets and to display advertisements via Google AdMob. This connection does not involve any personal user data.

📢 Advertising

SoulScript displays banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may use cookies or device identifiers to show relevant ads. This is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy. You can review Google's data practices at: policies.google.com/privacy

💾 Downloaded Images

When you tap the Download button, a quote image is saved directly to your device's local storage (gallery). This image stays on your device only. We have no access to it and do not upload it anywhere.

👶 Children's Privacy

SoulScript is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used this app, please note that no data has been collected regardless.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.
